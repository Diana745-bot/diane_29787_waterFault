

# Water Supply Fault Reporting and Repair Tracking System

## Project Overview

This project focuses on improving the management of public water supply faults through a database-based system. It addresses challenges related to reporting water leaks, tracking repair activities, assigning technicians, and monitoring fault resolution.

The system aims to improve fault reporting, maintenance coordination, accountability, and management decision-making.

## Phase I – Business Process Modeling

### 1. Selected Business Process

The selected business process is Water Supply Fault Reporting and Repair Tracking. It describes how residents report water supply problems, how Call Center Agents record faults, how Supervisors review and assign repairs, and how Repair Technicians resolve reported issues.

The process ensures that reported faults are properly recorded, monitored, repaired, and closed.
